Ioannina is known for its rich history, Ottoman-era architecture, and vibrant cultural life.
The city is built around Lake Pamvotida, also known as Lake Ioannina, which features a small inhabited island with monasteries dating back to the Byzantine period.
The Ioannina Castle is one of the oldest Byzantine fortresses in Greece and houses museums such as the Municipal Ethnographic Museum and the Byzantine Museum of Ioannina.
The municipality supports cultural events including the Ioannina Film Festival, traditional music performances, and exhibitions at the Municipal Gallery.
Tourism in Ioannina has grown due to its proximity to the Zagori villages and the Vikos Gorge, part of the Vikos–Aoos National Park.
Visitors often explore Ioannina’s old town, known for its cobbled streets, silver workshops, and Ottoman monuments.
