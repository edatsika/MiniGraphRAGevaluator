The economy of Ioannina is based on services, education, light manufacturing, and tourism.
The University of Ioannina is one of Greece’s major universities, hosting thousands of students from across the country and abroad.
It contributes significantly to the local economy and research development.
Small and medium enterprises dominate the local business landscape, particularly in retail, crafts, and food production.
The municipality encourages entrepreneurship through local development programs co-funded by the European Union.
Ioannina is also a regional center for healthcare and public administration, attracting workers from nearby towns in Epirus.
Recent years have seen infrastructure upgrades, including road connections to Igoumenitsa and Thessaloniki.
