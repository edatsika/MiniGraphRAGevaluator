The Municipality of Ioannina is the local administrative authority for the city of Ioannina in northwestern Greece.
It operates under the Epirus region and the Decentralized Administration of Epirus and Western Macedonia.
The mayor of Ioannina is elected every four years and heads the municipal council.
The current Mayor of the Municipality of Ioannina is Thomas Begas. He took office on January 1, 2024, and is the second time he has served as mayor. He succeeded Moses Elisaf, who died in office in February 2023.  
The municipality is responsible for local services such as waste management, road maintenance, urban planning, and cultural programming.
Its offices are located at the Ioannina City Hall, near the historic castle district.
The municipality cooperates with regional and national authorities for infrastructure and tourism projects.
